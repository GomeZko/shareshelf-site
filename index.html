The idea

Most things we buy are used only a few times

People in Tallinn buy tools, camping gear and electronics for one job, then store them for years. Borrowing from friends works, until the friend does not have it or you do not want to ask again.

How it works

Four steps, from search to return

1

Find it nearby

Search by item and see what neighbours have, with price, deposit and distance.

2

Book and pay

Choose the days and pay in the app. The deposit is held, not sent to the owner.

3

Pick up and check

Meet the owner, test the item together and tick the handover checklist.

4

Return and rate

The owner checks the item and taps “Accepted”. The deposit is released and you rate each other.

If you need something

  • Get it for a day or a weekend, close to home
  • Pay much less than buying it
  • No favours to ask, no storage needed

If you own something

  • Earn from camping, sports or photo gear that sits idle
  • Rent only to verified people, with a deposit
  • Check every item at return before closing the rental

Trust and safety

Built around the main worry: damage

Damage is what people worry about most when they rent from a stranger. So every rental follows the same clear rules.

Verified people

Every user confirms who they are before the first rental.

Deposit on every rental

The deposit covers small damage and is released only after the owner accepts the item.

Check at handover and return

A short checklist and photos at pickup make it clear what condition the item was in.

Two-way ratings

Renters and owners rate each other after every rental.

Items up to €500

At the start, item value is capped at €500 to keep the risk small.

Help with disputes

If something goes wrong, our team helps both sides reach a fair result.

FAQ

Questions and answers

How much does it cost?

The owner sets the daily price. ShareShelf adds a small service fee to each rental. Joining and listing items is free.

What if the item gets damaged?

Both sides check the item at handover and return. Small damage is covered by the deposit. In bigger cases, our team helps both sides agree on a fair solution.

How does the deposit work?

The deposit is held when you book and released after the owner confirms that the item came back in good condition.

Who can rent and lend?

Anyone aged 18 or over who confirms their identity. Verified users and two-way ratings keep the community safe.

What can I list?

Everyday items worth up to €500: tools, camping and sports gear, cameras, party equipment and more. Unsafe or illegal items are not allowed.

How do pickup and return work?

You agree on a time and place in the chat, meet the owner and check the item together. At return, the owner taps “Accepted” to close the rental.

When and where does ShareShelf launch?

We start with a pilot in Tallinn. Join the waitlist to get an invite as soon as your area opens.

Is there an app?

Not yet. You can try our clickable prototype today. It runs in the browser on your phone, so there is nothing to install.
